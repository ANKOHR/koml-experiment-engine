# Lessons

The domain needs a clear separation between a recorded acceptance criterion and a model-derived belief. A high prior mean does not substitute for enough recorded trials; this engine evaluates acceptance from observations and minimum counts separately.

Synthetic evidence validated the assumption that exact rational arithmetic can reconcile all planted counts and posterior means deterministically. Corrupting 18 successes changes totals and the affected verdict, validating that reports compute rather than reproduce an expectations file. Real-world accuracy and usefulness remain unmeasured.

Operating rule: declare cutoff dates, priors, provenance and acceptance criteria before evaluating results; preserve independent hand calculations and never equate synthetic correctness with operational value.
